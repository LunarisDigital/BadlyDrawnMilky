1. Hero section

   - Assets: all files labeled "1 Hero" (assets/Homepage), plus links forward into the
     "2 BI - M2 start Transition" clips at the end.
   - Layering: "1 Hero- M1 BG.mp4" is the background layer, always running underneath,
     looped seamlessly (no visible cut/jump at the loop point).
   - Sequence:
       a. "1 Hero- M1 start transition.mp4" plays first — starts off the hero section.
       b. Then settles into "1 Hero- M1 Loop.mp4" — this is when the welcome-message text
          slowly animates/comes onto the screen.
       c. On scroll down: "1 Hero- M1 to M2 transition.mp4" plays,
          with "1 Hero - M1 to M2 transition_erase action _ top layer_.mp4" layered on
          TOP of it (the scribble/wipe mask effect).
       d. Then "2 B I- M2 start Transition_.mp4" plays, together with its background
          plate "2 BI - M2 start transition bg.mp4" (linked/played together).
       e. Page/stage background color default: white, until "2 B I- M2 start Transition_"
          finishes playing (then presumably handed off to section 2's own background).
