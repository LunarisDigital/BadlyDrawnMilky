BADLYDRAWNMILKY — WEBSITE DESIGN DISCOVERY QUESTIONS
=====================================================

A list of questions to help nail down the creative direction, scope,
tone, and technical requirements of the BadlyDrawnMilky website
before any design work begins.


-----------------------------------------------------
1. VOICE, PERSPECTIVE & BRAND IDENTITY
-----------------------------------------------------

1.1  Whose voice should the site speak in?
     a) First-person, from Milky's perspective
        e.g. "Hi, I'm Milky. I bark, I nap, I get badly drawn."
     b) Third-person, framing Milky as the subject of the work
        e.g. "Milky is a dog. BadlyDrawnMilky is the animated series
        about him, drawn by [creator]."
     c) A blend — Milky narrates, but creator credits appear elsewhere
        (about page, footer, etc.)

1.2  Is the website primarily for:
     a) Milky (the character/IP)
     b) The creator/artist behind the animations
     c) Both, with a clear hierarchy

1.3  How "polished" should the website feel relative to the
     intentionally low-effort animation style?
     a) Match the rough, scribbled aesthetic
     b) Contrast it — clean, modern site as a backdrop for messy art
     c) Somewhere in between (polished structure, scribbled accents)

1.4  Tone of voice for copy: silly, sincere, deadpan, chaotic,
     wholesome, sarcastic — which adjectives feel right, and which
     are off-limits?

1.5  Are there any existing brand guidelines, color palettes, fonts,
     or logo files we should be working from?


-----------------------------------------------------
2. PURPOSE & GOALS OF THE WEBSITE
-----------------------------------------------------

2.1  What is the #1 thing you want the website to do?
     a) Be a "home base" / link hub for all social platforms
     b) Sell merchandise
     c) Showcase a portfolio/archive of animations
     d) Pitch to brands, agents, or media partners
     e) Something else

2.2  If a visitor only does ONE thing on the site, what should it be?
     (Watch a video? Buy a sticker? Follow on Instagram? Contact for collaboration?)

-----------------------------------------------------
3. CONTENT & SECTIONS
-----------------------------------------------------

3.1  Which of these sections do you want, and in what priority?
     [ ] Home / landing
     [ ] About Milky (the dog)
     [ ] About the creator
     [ ] Animation archive / gallery
     [ ] Latest / featured video
     [ ] Shop / merch
     [ ] Press & features
     [ ] Brand partnerships / "work with us"
     [ ] Contact
     [ ] Newsletter signup
     [ ] Blog / behind-the-scenes
     [ ] Fan art submissions
     [ ] FAQ
     [ ] Other: __________

3.2  Should there be a "sound library" — clips of the real Milky's
     barks/snorts/sounds — as its own feature?

3.3  Do you want a section that explains the *process* (how a real
     bark becomes an animation)?

3.4  Is there a story/origin section? How much of the creator's
     personal story should be public?

-----------------------------------------------------
4. SOCIAL & COMMUNITY INTEGRATION
-----------------------------------------------------

4.1  Which social platforms must be linked prominently?
     (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X/Twitter, Threads, Bluesky,
     Discord, etc.)

4.2  Should the latest posts from any of those platforms auto-embed
     on the site, or just be linked out?

4.3  Is there a community space (Discord, subreddit, etc.) that
     should be highlighted?

4.4  Do you want a fan submission feature (fan art, voice clips,
     drawings of Milky)? Moderated or open?

-----------------------------------------------------
6. VISUAL & INTERACTION DESIGN
-----------------------------------------------------

6.1  Should the site lean heavily into animation (lots of motion,
     wiggling drawings, audio cues), or stay mostly static for
     performance/accessibility?

6.2  Are there "signature" interactions you'd love to see?
     (e.g. cursor turns into a paw, Milky barks on hover, hand-drawn
     scribbles draw themselves as you scroll)

6.3  Should the site play sound by default, on hover, on click, or
     never (sound off unless user opts in)?

6.4  Any websites / brands you love the look or feel of, that we
     should treat as references?

6.5  Any websites / styles you specifically dislike?

6.6  Light mode, dark mode, or both? Any specific palette in mind?

6.7  Typography preference — hand-lettered, system fonts, a specific
     typeface, or a mix?

6.8  Logo — does one exist? Does it need refinement or a new mark?

-----------------------------------------------------
7. THE BIG-PICTURE QUESTIONS
-----------------------------------------------------

7.1  In one sentence, how would you describe BadlyDrawnMilky to
     someone who's never heard of it?

7.2  In one word, how should a visitor feel when they leave the
     website?

7.3  What would make you look at the finished site and say "yes,
     that's exactly right"?

7.4  What would make you look at it and say "no, that's not Milky"?

7.5  Where do you see BadlyDrawnMilky in 2-3 years, and should the
     website be designed to grow with that vision (TV show, book,
     plushie line, etc.) or stay focused on what it is today?


-----------------------------------------------------
END OF QUESTIONS
